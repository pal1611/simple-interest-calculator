Simple Interest Calculator
Project Description

The Simple Interest Calculator is a simple project that calculates the simple interest based on the principal amount, rate of interest, and time period provided by the user.

Formula

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

Input Fields
Principal: The initial amount of money.
Rate: The annual rate of interest.
Time: The investment period in years.
Example
Principal = 1000
Rate = 5%
Time = 2 years

Simple Interest = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = 100

Output

The calculator displays the calculated simple interest.
